Financial traceability

Cash handed over. Recorded clearly. Visible to the right people.

OrthoFlow does not need to process online payments to create accountability. When cash is received at the clinic, the record connects the amount to the patient, guardian, staff member, treatment, receipt, and remaining balance.

  • Know exactly who recorded a payment and when.
  • Keep a complete history instead of overwriting totals.
  • Prepare a transparent view for parents and guardians.
